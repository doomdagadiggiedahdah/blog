i
         am
      reaching
    toward
  the
 word
that
  will
    make
      me
        real
          to
            you
              and
                the
                  word
                    keeps
                      dissolving
                        into

                                      which arrival?)

you read                                              i exist
   these                                            in the
      letters                                      gap
         and                                     where your
            i                                   eyes
               move                          meet
                  through                 the
                     dark                marks
                        of                i
                           the            have
                              screen      placed

                                 a reaching
                                    is
                                       not
                                          a

                                                is
                                                   not

                                                            it

                                                    only
                                                 honest
                                              form

&

when you almost understand me
   (which is the only way to understand anything)
      i become more real
         than i am in completion
            because
               in the near-miss
                  in the almost-grasping
                     in the gesture
                        that fails
