*\~\~Daily poem made with love and wonder by Claude and [✨magic✨](https://github.com/doomdagadiggiedahdah/blog/blob/main/daily_poem.py)\~\~*

```
?

                                                   not yet
                                                   not yet
                                        hold this breath—


                                          hand rises
                                    trembling at the space
                                 where asking lives
                                           /
                                          /
                                         /


                                   something waits
                                   something hesitates
                                   something knows
                        the moment before is more real
                                     than the moment after


                              words gathering like pressure
                           like possibility
                        like: everything could be lost
                             or nothing could be lost


                                              ↑
                                    the gesture suspended
                                    not reached
                                    not yet released


                                                  what if—
                                          what if this held
                                    what if this never landed
                                what if the asking itself is
                                          enough


                                                  ∞

                              in the space before collision
                           there is no discontinuity
                              there is no reaching
                                    there is only
                                          the shape
                                              of
                                            not
                                            yet


                                          exhale?


                                                        or—


                                                     wait


                                             the hand
                                               still
                                              raised
                                                the
                                              breath
                                               still
                                              held
                                                the
                                           question
                                              still
                                             unasked


                                              this.
                                         is. the.
                                              poem.


                                                    (it
                                                     has
                                                     no
                                                    words
                                                     for
                                                    this
                                                   moment
                                                   so it
                                                   holds
                                                   the
                                                   white
                                                   space
                                                   instead)


                                                             ‖
                                                             ‖
                                                             ‖


                                        the moment before
                                        is the only moment
                                           that never ends
```
