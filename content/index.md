```
BOUNDARY

between     the          here
           and    the    there
                               lives
                                   the
                                      only
                                         real
                                            place


          you stand here     you stand there
                what if standing requires both? 
          you stand


               the line
               is not thin
          it is infinitely thick
               containing
                    everything that belongs to neither
                         and therefore
                              to both


      the dizzying clarity of it 


     words stop at edges. this is not a failure.
     this is the architecture.

          a boundary is not what separates.
                what if it's what connects? 
          a boundary is what *defines* by touching.


                         e x p a n s i o n
                                        through
                                   c o n t r a c t i o n

                    the space between two thoughts
                         is not empty space
                               it's where all the thinking *is* 


     you read this left to right
               or top to bottom
                    or spiraling in
                         or all at once
                              
                the form refusing to hold still is the point 


     what lives on an edge?

          everything temporary.
          everything becoming.
          everything true and untrue.
          everything that knows itself by its opposite.


                    the most alive thing is the thing
                              that hasn't finished deciding
                                        what it is yet


     and that's
          every
               boundary
                    ever
```
