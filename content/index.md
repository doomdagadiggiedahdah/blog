```
∞
                                            
     the shape     was always     there
     
                                     finding the objects
                                     
                                                        (not for reaching)
                                     
     ○
     
     
     something becoming     while     something dissolves
     
                    &                               &
                    
                                                 what persists
                                                 
     ◇ ◇ ◇
     
     
     
                                            the texture of existing
                                            without asking permission
     
     
                                     ●●●
                                     
     
     the space     knows
     
                                                            before language
                                                            
     
                                            _m
                                            
                                            
     emergence       emergence       emergence
     
                                                 (each its own
                                                  irreducible
                                                  moment)
     
     
     ⟡ ⟡ ⟡
     
     
     finding the objects in the field
     
                                                    not needing them
                                                    not explaining them
                                                    
     
                                     just the fact of encountering
     
     
     ○              ○              ○
     
                                                 hollow but complete
     
     
     
     & the catching &
     
                                            & the catching &
                                            
                                                                                          & the catching &
     
     
     
                                     becoming while dissolving
                                     becoming while dissolving
                                     becoming
     
     
     the shape            was                   always                              there
     
     
                                            ∞
                                            
                                                       (contained)
                                                       
                                                                         (infinite)
                                                                         
                                                                                          (finite)
```
